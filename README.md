# 📊 Churn Analysis & Customer Intelligence

## 📌 Overview

A **Data Analytics project** focused on analyzing customer churn, identifying high-risk customers, and generating actionable business insights.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Python** — Pandas, NumPy
* **SQL / SQLite** — Data extraction & analysis
* **Matplotlib & Seaborn** — Data visualization

## 🔄 Workflow

```text
SQL Database
     ↓
Data Extraction
     ↓
Data Cleaning
     ↓
Feature Engineering
     ↓
EDA & Analysis
     ↓
Visualization
     ↓
Business Insights
```

## 📈 Key Analysis

* Churn & retention rate
* Churn by subscription plan
* Churn by state
* Customer tenure
* Revenue & ARPU
* Monthly vs Annual churn
* Complaints & escalations

## 💡 Key Insights

* **Churn Rate:** 28.6%
* **Retention Rate:** 71.4%
* **Revenue Loss:** 18%
* **Monthly Churn:** 55.6%
* **Annual Churn:** 8.3%
* **Most affected state:** Karnataka
* **Highest churn:** Basic subscription plan
* **Highest churn period:** September 2024

## 🚀 Recommendations

* Investigate the reasons for high churn in Karnataka.
* Review Basic plan changes and pricing.
* Analyze competitor activity.
* Focus on **High & Medium churn-risk customers**.
* Proactively contact customers through email, SMS, or calls.
